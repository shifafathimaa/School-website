Admission Procedure

Everything you need to know about joining the Azeeziya family

Eligibility

Admission Criteria

Admissions are open to all students who meet the age and academic eligibility requirements.

Nursery / LKG

Age: 3 – 4 years as of June 1st of the admission year

UKG / Class I

Age: 5 – 6 years as of June 1st; prior schooling preferred

Class II – VIII

Transfer Certificate from previous school required; written interaction may be conducted

Class IX – X

Subject to seat availability; academic record assessment and interaction required

Step by Step

Admission Process

1

Submit Application

Complete the online application form at Apply Online or collect the paper form from the school office. Fill in all student, guardian, and academic details accurately. Pay the non-refundable application fee.

2

Document Verification

Submit all required documents to the school office for verification. Originals must be presented for checking. Incomplete applications will not be processed.

3

Student Interaction / Assessment

For Classes I and above, a brief informal interaction or age-appropriate assessment is conducted to understand the child's readiness. Parents may also be called for an orientation meeting.

4

Admission Confirmation

Selected candidates will be notified via phone/email/post within 5–7 working days. An admission offer letter will be issued specifying the deadline for fee payment.

5

Fee Payment & Enrollment

Pay the admission fee and first instalment of tuition fee within the stipulated deadline to confirm the seat. Late payment may result in forfeiture of the offered seat.

6

Welcome & Orientation

Attend the New Student Orientation Day to collect your ID card, uniform details, timetable, and meet your class teachers. Welcome to the Azeeziya family! 🎉
