Welcome to Our School

A Place Where Knowledge Meets Character

Azeeziya English Medium School stands as a beacon of academic excellence in Chithari, Kanhangad, Kerala. Affiliated to CBSE New Delhi (School No. 931186), we have grown into an institution that balances academic rigor with character building and holistic development.

Our dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art facilities, and student-centric approach ensure that every child who walks through our doors receives the best possible foundation for a bright future.

A Word From Our CEO

Education is not merely the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire. At Azeeziya, we strive to ignite that fire of curiosity, compassion, and excellence in every child.

Our school has been a pillar of the Kanhangad community for over two decades. We remain committed to academic rigor while nurturing the emotional and social growth of each student. Our teachers are not just educators — they are mentors, guides, and the architects of a brighter tomorrow. I warmly welcome you to be a part of the Azeeziya family.
