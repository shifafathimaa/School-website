About Azeeziya School

Azeeziya English Medium School is a proud educational institution situated in Chithari, Kanhangad, Kerala. Founded with the vision of providing quality, affordable, and value-based education, we have been shaping young minds for over two decades.

Affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), our school offers education from Nursery to Class X. We pride ourselves on a balanced approach that nurtures intellectual curiosity, spiritual growth, physical fitness, and social responsibility.

Our dedicated team of qualified and experienced educators is committed to creating an inclusive, supportive, and stimulating learning environment where every student thrives.
